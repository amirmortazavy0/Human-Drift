# Key Questions

These are the sub-questions that sit under the main research question. Add to this list as you discover new angles. Do not try to answer them all — use them to guide what evidence you look for.

---

## Core question
> What are the most common and costly ways large industrial organizations drift from their annual plans, and why do current systems fail to detect or explain that drift early — especially between Planning, Production, QA/QC and Technical teams?

---

## Sub-questions

### About the drift itself
- At what point in the year does drift typically become visible?
- Is drift gradual or does it happen in sudden jumps?
- Which department is most often the origin point of drift?
- Is drift more common in certain industries or plant sizes?

### About detection failure
- What data do organizations already have that could detect drift earlier?
- Why does that data not produce early warnings?
- Who is responsible for detecting drift — and is that responsibility clear?
- What does "early enough" actually mean in an industrial context?

### About cross-department dynamics
- How do Planning, Production, QA, and Technical teams currently communicate about plan status?
- Where does information get stuck or distorted between departments?
- Who has authority to escalate when drift is detected?

### About current tools
- What software do organizations use to track plan vs actual?
- Where do those tools fail?
- What do practitioners complain about most?
