## What it is

A paid database of 95,800 licensed contractors in Iran across all industries, maintained by SafdelAcademy. A free sample (Oil & Gas category, 20 companies) was downloaded and saved in this folder.

## What each record contains

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| نام شرکت | Company name |
| شناسه ملی | National company ID |
| مدیرعامل | CEO name |
| حوزه فعالیت | Activity domain |
| رتبه پیمانکاری | Contractor grade (1 = highest, 5 = lowest) |
| تلفن ثابت | Phone number |
| آدرس | Full address |
| توضیحات | Specialization details, shareholders, sub-grades |
| وضعیت گواهینامه | Certificate status: معتبر (valid) / منقضی (expired) |

## Why it's relevant

Contractors work *inside* large industrial organizations and sit directly in the coordination gap between departments. They see dysfunction from the outside with no political reason to protect the client's failures. Grade 1 contractors working in Oil & Gas and Petrochemicals are likely to have experienced planning drift firsthand.

## When to use it

**Stage 5 — Conversations.** Not before.

When ready:
1. Filter by **وضعیت: معتبر** (valid certificate only)
2. Filter by **رتبه: 1** (highest grade — work on largest projects)
3. Filter by relevant industries (نفت و گاز، پتروشیمی، فولاد)
4. Look up CEOs or operations directors on LinkedIn
5. Reach out for 20–30 minute conversations about planning and coordination problems

## Free alternative (no cost, use now if needed)

Search LinkedIn for `پیمانکار نفت و گاز` or `پیمانکار پتروشیمی` to find individual practitioners without purchasing the list.
