## What it is

A comprehensive map of the shareholder relationship chain (زنجیره ارتباطات سهامداران) of Iranian petrochemical companies. Shows which entities own shares in which petrochemical companies — state funds, banks, holding groups, investment companies, and their subsidiaries.

## Why it's relevant to the research

This is not operational evidence of planning drift — it is **structural context** for why drift is hard to solve.

Key observation: Major petrochemical plants often have 5–10 institutional shareholders, each with their own reporting demands and KPI frameworks. When a company like MAPNA Group (مپنا) appears as the dominant shareholder across multiple subsidiaries with shared boards, organizational alignment between those entities becomes structurally complex. Planning, reporting, and accountability flow through ownership structures that don't match operational realities.

This explains *why* early detection fails at the organizational level: no single owner has full operational visibility, and each shareholder's reporting demands create different and sometimes conflicting definitions of "plan vs actual."

## When to use it

- **Stage 4** — when writing the one-page brief, use this to illustrate the structural complexity of the Iranian industrial sector as context for the problem
- **Stage 5** — when identifying who to approach, cross-reference holding groups visible in this map with the contractor list to find the right entry points
