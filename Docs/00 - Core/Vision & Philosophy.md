# Vision & Philosophy — Human Drift

---

## What Human Drift is

Human Drift is a research project and an emerging product.

**As research:** An investigation into how and why people and organizations drift from what they planned — and why existing systems fail to detect that drift early enough to act on it.

**As product:** A context-aware autonomous operating system for human intentions. It detects when something that matters to a person is quietly disappearing from their life, distinguishes intentional change from unconscious drift, and maintains the integrity of intentions over time.

The long-term vision: something like Jarvis — focused entirely on planning, execution, and drift prevention. Not a productivity tool. Not a habit tracker. A system that holds the through-line of what a person decided mattered.

---

## The problem Human Drift solves

People do not consciously abandon the things they care about.

Life gradually replaces their attention.

A person may start learning something, building something, pursuing something — and then life becomes busy. Months later they realize: "When did I stop being that person?"

This happens to individuals. It happens to organizations. It happens across every domain where intention meets the complexity of real life.

Current systems — calendars, task managers, project software, ERP systems — track tasks. They do not track intentions. They do not detect drift. They do not distinguish between "I deliberately stopped" and "I got distracted and forgot."

Human Drift does.

---

## The product philosophy — non-negotiable

**Programs, not tasks.**
Human Drift manages ongoing intentions with duration, context, and meaning. Not things to check off.

**Intentions survive time.**
Inactivity is not abandonment. The system preserves what the person cared about until there is clear evidence of a deliberate decision to move on.

**Context before interruption.**
The system observes before it acts. A poorly timed interruption is worse than silence.

**Auditable autonomy.**
Every autonomous action the system takes is visible and explainable. No hidden behavior. The user can always ask: "What did you do, and why?"

**Observe first, infer second.**
Pattern recognition requires time and multiple signals. The system does not label something as drift after one missed day.

**No hardcoding. Ever.**
Every route, station, schedule, threshold, rule, and configuration is user-defined. The system adapts to the user — never the reverse.

**The goal is continuity of meaning, not productivity.**
Human Drift is not an optimization tool. It helps a person remain connected to what matters to them across the natural chaos of life.

---

## What Human Drift protects against

- Becoming a guilt machine
- Becoming a habit tracker with a different name
- Treating every life change as a problem to fix
- Optimizing behavior rather than preserving meaning
- Autonomous actions the user cannot understand or reverse
- Any hardcoded assumption about how a person lives

## The two threads

**Research thread** → `01 - Research/`
Proves the problem is real. Collects evidence of industrial and human plan-execution drift. Identifies why current systems fail. Builds the intellectual foundation for what Human Drift needs to become.

**Development thread** → `04 - Development/`
Builds the proof of concept. Starts with Journey-001 (train performance study). Progresses from manual data collection to autonomous context detection.

These threads are not parallel — they feed each other. Research discoveries shape product requirements. Product discoveries generate new research questions.
