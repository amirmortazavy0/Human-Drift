# Vision Horizon — Human Drift

This file exists for one purpose: to capture big ideas without acting on them prematurely.

When an idea lands here, it is safe. It will not be forgotten.
When the current phase is complete, this file gets reviewed.
Until then, nothing here gets built.

---

## Rule

One line per idea. No specs. No features. No architecture.
If an idea needs more than one line, it's not ready to be here yet — put it in the Inbox.

---

## Captured ideas

- General route logging for any trip type — friend's house, walking, driving, not just trains
- Walking distance and duration tracking over a month
- Phone usage time logging tied to goal consistency (e.g. "I decided to study math — am I actually on my phone instead?")
- Drift detection for learning goals: "You haven't studied math in 18 days. Was that intentional?"
- Any-mode commute: train, car, walk, bike — same data model, different context signals
- Voice capture: "Hey [app name], I'm starting a trip to X" — no tapping required
- Natural language program definition: user describes what they want in plain language, app translates it into a Program
- Social drift: "You haven't seen [friend] in 3 weeks — was that intentional?"
- Multi-user programs: shared drift detection for teams or couples
- Industrial/organizational drift detection (the original research question — this becomes a separate product or enterprise layer)
- The app as a general AGI-like operating system for human intentions, not just commute tracking
